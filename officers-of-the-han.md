## 1. Overview
- **The series' second original Three Kingdoms game.** Owner, 2026-11-07: "build a spiritual successor to the game of romance of the three kingdoms 8". It is not a port or a recreation: you play one officer in a living world (free officer to ruler), with our own rules, names of systems, text and art. Same posture as three-kingdoms-tactics (its D10): historical names from the public-domain novel and the record, stat values set by our own rubric from the record (`12-data.md` §3), never copied from any game.
- Owner decisions 2026-11-07, asked before the owner slept (two multiple-choice batches, 4 + 3; do not re-ask): new repo the owner creates (D1); **new hex field battles**, chosen over reusing the tactics square-grid battles (D2); full map, one scenario, play as any officer (D3); **190, the coalition against Dong Zhuo** (D4); presentation concept **Han lacquerware**: vermilion on black lacquer, cloud-scroll bands, cities as bronze mirrors, letters on silk (D5); all four pillars in the first playable: personal ties, duels and debates, novel events, create your own officer (D6); overnight run approved for specs, engines and a first playable, about 5-6M tokens (D7).
- Defaults taken: sibling stack; 2D SVG map with 3D R3F battles; monthly turns with 30 days per officer; posts Ruler, Prefect, Officer, Wanderer (Viceroy later); the AI never executes the player's officer; portraits copied from the tactics game where officers match, a lacquer medallion otherwise; saves per rule 22 under `oth-save`; no audio until a session on the owner's PC.

## 4. Lessons for the series
- *(added 2026-11-07, officers-of-the-han)* **Have the owner create the new repo before they leave.** A cloud session cannot create repos, and the auto-mode classifier refused pushing the new game's unrelated history to a session branch of a sibling repo as a stopgap. Work stays committed locally until the repo exists, so put "create the empty repo and enable Pages (Source: GitHub Actions)" in the kickoff message, not after the questions.
- *(added 2026-11-07, officers-of-the-han)* **Split an officer-play strategy game into separate engines joined by setup/outcome types.** The world hands a `BattleSetup`, `DuelSetup` or `DebateSetup` to its engine as a pending decision and validates the returned outcome. That lets the world and the contests be built at the same time with no shared files, and AI-vs-AI contests auto-resolve by formula in the world, so the sim doesn't depend on the battle engine.
- *(added 2026-11-07, officers-of-the-han)* **Run a data agent's output past a verify agent with sources before building on it.** The M1 agent filled 195 officers without web research despite rule 4; a second agent with curl to Wikipedia found 56 wrong dates or courtesy names and about 10 wrong 190 seats (`docs/handoff/m1b-verify.md`). Ask for `source` per row in the brief and budget the verify pass up front.
- *(added 2026-11-07, officers-of-the-han)* **Two screens built in separate worktrees collide on generic CSS class names.** The world and battle stylesheets both used `.hud`, `.ticker`, `.actions` and `.chip`; each looked right alone (the lab never loads `world.css`) and broke only when the battle mounted inside the world. Prefix each screen's classes from the start, and make the integration stage play a contest through the real client, not the lab (`docs/handoff/m3c-integ.md`).
- *(added 2026-11-07, officers-of-the-han)* **Pass a saved state to a screen once, not as a live prop.** The world handed the current saved battle to `BattleScreen` as `initial` on every render; each autosave re-ran its setup and reset the AI switch, log and camera. Hand it over on mount.
- *(added 2026-11-07, officers-of-the-han)* **A headless sim catches a lost opening story.** The first world AI declared 13-15 wars in month 1 of 190, so the coalition against Dong Zhuo never formed. A rule (no AI wars in the first 3 months; the coalition first) plus a `newGame` test fixed it; check the first year of a historical scenario in the sim, not only the 10-year averages.
- *(added 2026-11-07, officers-of-the-han)* **Fixing a historical runaway can freeze the map instead.** Once Dong Zhuo's house stopped absorbing neighbours, about 18 of 23 factions were still standing at 200 and battles fell from about 350 a year to about 30. The AI's war count also jumped sharply with one constant (war chance 70% gives 0.42 wars per faction-year, 80% gives 0.96). Track the number of factions and the battles per year alongside the target metric, and measure on 20-30 seeds (`docs/spec/13-balance.md`).
- *(added 2026-11-07, officers-of-the-han)* **A cloud session cannot reach the GPU PC.** Build the generation runners, prompt tables and in-game players against fake studios in the cloud, and write a runbook for a session on the PC. When the owner asks for generated art or audio from a cloud session, say so in the first reply. Sharing the PC folder through Remote Control is the other route.
